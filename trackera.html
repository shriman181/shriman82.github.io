The problem

Trade compliance is
broken by design.

Every cross-border shipment forces your team through a gauntlet of manual classification, shifting duty tables, and compliance risk — while regulators keep moving the goalposts.

📋
40,000 HS Codes, Zero Clarity
The Harmonized System spans over 40,000 codes. Expert classifiers disagree on over 30% of products. Misclassification triggers delayed shipments, fines, and CBP audits — often years later.
💸
Brokers Charge for Opacity
Licensed customs brokers charge $150–$500 per classification. High-volume importers spend hundreds of thousands annually for outcomes they can't verify, audit, or improve.
🔄
Rules Change Constantly
WCO updates HS codes every five years. Country-level tariff schedules change faster. A valid classification in January may carry surcharges by March — and your ERP won't tell you.
🚨
Sanctions Screening is Manual
OFAC, EU, UN and BIS denied-party lists update daily. A single sanctioned counterparty in your supply chain exposes you to criminal liability. Manual screening doesn't scale.
🤝
FTA Benefits Left Unclaimed
Most SMEs systematically miss duty savings available under USMCA, CPTPP, EU-Korea, and other FTAs because eligibility rules are too complex to apply without specialist counsel.
📊
No Audit Trail, No Defense
When customs authorities question a classification decision years later, importers must reconstruct the rationale from email threads and spreadsheets — or pay the assessment.
The solution

One platform.
Full compliance stack.

Trackera's AI engine covers every layer of trade compliance — from first-pass classification to duty calculation, screening, and FTA optimization — with documented reasoning at every step.

01 HS Code Classification

Paste a product description, upload a spec sheet, or push via API. Trackera's multi-model AI returns the correct 6–10 digit HS code with confidence score, WCO classification rationale, and full audit log — in under 3 seconds. Bulk-classify entire SKU catalogs overnight.

02 Duty & Tax Calculator

Landed cost calculations across 220+ jurisdictions, updated daily. Factor in origin country, shipping terms, product value, and applicable trade remedies — anti-dumping, safeguards, countervailing duties — for a true total landed cost before you commit to the shipment.

03 Sanctions & Denied-Party Screening

Screen counterparties, vessels, and goods against OFAC SDN, EU Consolidated, UN Security Council, BIS Entity List, and 40+ additional lists — updated within hours of publication. Fuzzy-matching logic catches name variations. Full audit trail for regulatory defense.

04 FTA Eligibility Engine

Automatically evaluate shipment eligibility under USMCA, CPTPP, ASEAN, EU-Korea, AfCFTA, and 60+ active FTAs. Map rules-of-origin requirements, generate certificate-of-origin guidance, and quantify duty savings you're currently leaving on the table.
